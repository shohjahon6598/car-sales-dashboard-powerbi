# Car Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI report analysing used car sales in the US, 2014-2015.
The dataset and the task were provided as part of a course assignment.

## Report pages

**1. Overview** - total revenue, cars sold, average price, average condition, average odometer, and % vs MMR, with revenue trend, top 5 brands, sales by body type, and sales by state.

**2. Brand and Model** - average selling price by make, seller revenue contribution, and a detailed table with price deviation from MMR.

**3. Model Detail** - a drill-through page for one brand (Ford): price status vs MMR, average price by trim, price vs MMR by month, and cars sold by state.
